Ask away
Who is this for?

Aspiring creators, working creators, and founders building audience trust.

Do I need equipment or followers?

No. A smartphone is enough to start. Studio gear (Sony FX, lighting, audio) is provided on the lot.

Do I shoot at Ramanaidu Studios?

Yes. Weeks 9 and 10 are full-day production shoots on Stage 01 and Bay 04.

Is AI included?

Yes. Ethical AI for ideation, transcription indexing, and editing automation, keeping your creative judgment in charge.

How does payment work?

The application is free. Tuition is paid only after formal selection.

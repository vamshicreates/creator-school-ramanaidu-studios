The orange economy

How the creator economy will shape India

The orange economy is the part of the economy built on creativity: content, film, music, animation, gaming, comics and live events.

In the Union Budget 2026–27, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman named it a growth priority and proposed AVGC Content Creator Labs in 15,000 secondary schools and 500 colleges, set up with the Indian Institute of Creative Technologies, Mumbai.

15,500

Content creator labs proposed for schools and colleges

20 lakh

Professionals India’s AVGC sector may need by 2030

2–2.5M

Monetised creators in India today

$1T+

Consumer spending creators could influence by 2030, up from $350–400B now

What you should know

  • Creators already influence more than 30% of Indian consumers’ buying decisions.
  • Revenue for India’s creator ecosystem could grow from $20–25 billion to $100–125 billion by 2030.
  • Today, about 90% of that revenue comes from brands, so the skills to work with brands matter.

Sources: Union Budget 2026–27 speech (1 Feb 2026), as reported by ANI, Business Today and Inc42; BCG, “From Content to Commerce: Mapping India’s Creator Economy” (WAVES 2025).
